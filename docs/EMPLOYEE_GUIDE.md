# Employee guide

## Getting started

1. Open the email from your organization titled "Reset your password" and set your password. (HR may instead give you a one-time setup link.)
2. Open the app address in **Chrome (Android)** or **Safari (iPhone)** and sign in with your work email.
3. Read and accept the **location privacy notice**. Location is collected only when you tap an attendance button — never in the background.
4. Optional: install the app.
   - **iPhone (Safari):** Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.
   - **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.

## Registering your phone (if your organization requires it)

1. On your **own** phone, open **Profile → Registered phone** and tap **Register this phone**, then **Confirm with fingerprint, face or PIN**.
2. Your phone needs a screen lock. The check happens on your phone; ClockUp never receives your fingerprint, face or PIN.
3. When clocking in you'll tap **Confirm & submit** and unlock with your fingerprint, face or PIN.
4. New phone? Register it the same way; HR must approve it before you can use it. Only one phone per employee, and a phone registered to someone else cannot be registered to you.

## Clocking in and out

1. On **Today**, check your schedule and tap the large button. It only shows the next valid action (Clock In / Clock Out, or AM In / AM Out / PM In / PM Out).
2. The first time, the app explains why it needs your location; tap **Continue** and choose **Allow**.
3. The app shows your **accuracy** and **distance** from your workplace. If accuracy is poor, move near a window or outdoors and tap **Retry location**.
4. Tap **Submit**. Your attendance is recorded only when you see the **Attendance recorded** receipt with the official server time and location.

If the button is unavailable, the message under it explains why (for example, too early, shift closed, or already completed).

### If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Location permission is blocked | Follow the on-screen steps to allow location for this site, then tap **Retry**. |
| Location not precise enough / too old | Move to a spot with a clearer sky view, wait a few seconds, and retry. |
| Outside your authorized area | Move closer to your workplace. If you are on official business, submit a **fieldwork** request. |
| You are offline | Attendance needs internet and is never saved for later. Reconnect and try again, or submit a correction request. |
| Not yet confirmed | Your connection dropped. Tap **Check status**; if it was not recorded, tap **Retry same request** — it cannot create a duplicate. |

## History

**History** shows each day's status, times, late/undertime, missing entries, and whether a record was corrected. Tap a day for details.

## Requests

Use **Requests → New request** for:
- **Missing clock-in/out** or **Incorrect attendance** — give the correct date, action, time and an explanation.
- **Official business / fieldwork** — the time window and either the site location or a location exemption.
- **Work from home** — the dates/times and your **home location**: while at home, tap *Use my current location (I am at home)*. Once approved, you can clock in and out only within that radius during the approved period, and your records show "Work from home".
- **Overtime approval** — file it **before** working overtime (up to 31 days ahead), with the planned minutes. Once filed — even while pending — after your regular Clock Out / PM Out the button shows **Overtime In**, then **Overtime Out** when you finish (accepted up to 12 hours later). Overtime counts as approved only after approval, up to the planned minutes.
- **Exception explanation**.

Requests take effect only after your supervisor or HR approves them. Your explanation alone does not record attendance.

## Your privacy

- Location is captured once per attendance action, not continuously.
- Precise coordinates are visible to you and HR only.
- You cannot change your role, department, schedule, workplaces or approved records; contact HR.
- **Profile → Sign out** when using a shared device, and untick "Keep me signed in" when signing in on one.
